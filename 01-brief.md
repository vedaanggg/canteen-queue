# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends customers sit 1–2 hours in the salon without knowing their turn.
Some leave; others argue about who came first. We need a fair, visible queue
where customers can join from their phone and see their wait time.

## Users
- **Customer:** joins the queue, tracks position and wait, gets called.
- **Receptionist / owner:** adds walk-ins, calls the next customer, handles
  no-shows and cancellations.
- **Barber:** starts and finishes services, goes on break.
- **Owner:** manages services, barbers on duty and open/close of the queue.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from a phone: pick up to 3 services and a barber (or "Any")
2. Live status: token, position, estimated wait, "Start coming" alert and
   "It's your turn!" alert
3. Reception dashboard: add walk-ins, call next, mark no-show, cancel
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, on break toggle
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber + next 5 tokens
6. Owner settings: services (time, price, on/off), barbers on duty, open/close
   online joining, today's stats
7. English and Gujarati labels with a language toggle
8. Staff screens protected by a simple shared PIN

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, loyalty
points, multiple branches, customer accounts or login, reviews.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal + gold accent, clean, mobile-first.
Labels in English and Gujarati.
